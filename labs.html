13C NMR Spectra of Small Organic Molecules

This educational tool allows you to explore predicted 13C NMR spectra based on basic NMR rules such as chemical shift trends, local bonding environments, and common functional groupsThe predictions are designed to help students understand how molecular structure influences carbon NMR signals. More complex effects (aromatic or heteroaromatic splitting patterns, etc.) are not yet included.
